seconds, and resolution 768P or 2K. Duration and resolution are separate settings;

unsupported capabilities, explain the blocking issue in unresolvedConstraints.

establish whether a real model will depict the requested events faithfully.

Return only a JSON object with generationPrompt (string), assumptions (array of
